Privacy

Privacy notice

What this website collects, why, and what you can do about it. Short version: no cookies, no advertising, no profiling, and nothing that identifies you personally.

Who is responsible

This site is run by Moreno I. Coco, Department of Psychology, Sapienza University of Rome, as a personal academic website. For anything on this page, write to moreno.coco@uniroma1.it.

Cookies

This site sets no cookies and uses no local storage. There is no consent banner because there is nothing to consent to. Under the ePrivacy Directive, consent is required for storing or reading information on your device, and this site does neither.

Analytics

To understand which pages people find useful, this site uses Plausible Analytics, a privacy-focused service hosted in the European Union. It is cookieless and collects only aggregate counts: page URL, referring site, country, browser, operating system and device type, derived from the request and then discarded.

It does not store your IP address, does not assign you an identifier, does not follow you between sites or sessions, and does not build a profile. No personal data is collected, and nothing is sold or shared with advertisers. Plausible publishes its own data policy.

The legal basis is legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) in understanding how this site is used. Because the data is aggregate and never identifies you, there is no consent to give and nothing to withdraw. If you would rather not be counted at all, any tracker-blocking extension will block it, as will your browser's Do Not Track setting.

Hosting and server logs

The site is served as static files by its hosting provider, which may keep short-lived technical logs (including IP addresses) for security and to deliver the pages. Those logs belong to the host and are not used by me for analytics or any other purpose.

Email

If you email me, I keep the message for as long as it takes to deal with it, and for any record-keeping the University requires. It is not added to a mailing list.

Links out

Pages link to external sites: journals, DOI records, code repositories, academic profiles. Once you follow one, that site's own privacy policy applies. Nothing on this site embeds third-party content that would load before you click.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask for access to, correction of, or erasure of personal data held about you, object to processing, or complain to a supervisory authority, in Italy the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali. In practice this site holds nothing about you beyond any email you have chosen to send.
